## TUM RGB-D data

Source: [TUM RGB-D benchmark](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset). License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**, unless otherwise stated by the authors.

Credit: Jürgen Sturm, Nikolas Engelhard, Felix Endres, Wolfram Burgard and Daniel Cremers, *A Benchmark for the Evaluation of RGB-D SLAM Systems*, IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2012.

The four source archives remain local and excluded from Git. Published trajectories, numerical analyses, plots and any small visual replay excerpts are derived from those recordings. Visual excerpts are resized and annotated; trajectory estimates, perturbations and ORB overlays are generated by DepthTrack. These derivatives retain attribution under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement by the original authors is implied.

The camera-motion unit-test scenes are synthetic and independently generated by the project; they are not TUM recordings.
